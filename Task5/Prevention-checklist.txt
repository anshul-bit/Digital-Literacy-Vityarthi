🛡️  Stay Safe Online Checklist

✅ General Safety Tips: 
 Use strong, unique passwords for all accounts
 Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible
 Never click on unknown or suspicious links
 Verify the identity of callers before sharing any information
 Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive activities
 Regularly update apps and devices for security patches
💸 UPI / Financial Safety (IMPORTANT)
 Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money (that’s a scam)
 Do not approve unknown payment requests
 Avoid installing screen-sharing apps when asked by strangers
 Always verify payment requests before accepting
🚨 Reporting Channels
 Report fraud at National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
 Call Helpline: 1930 immediately for financial fraud
